# ENDS

**ENDS** is a single-player, narrative-driven game set in the Pembridge Estate in London, where players take on the role of Rahim, a young man navigating life alongside his friends Sonny and Reece as their world changes around them. With a mix of sandbox and RPG elements, the game delivers a grounded crime drama that explores themes of brotherhood, family, and loyalty through a choice-driven storyline.

## Gameplay Features
- **Intense Melee Combat** – Engage in hand-to-hand encounters with combo mechanics, grappling, and environmental attacks.
- **Fluid Movement & Traversal** – Enhanced with motion matching, foot locking, and orientation warping for high-quality animation.
- **Exploration & Transportation** – Traverse the city using bicycles, mopeds, various vehicles, or take the train for a faster route.
- **Immersive World** – Real-life brands, dynamic side activities, and chance encounters shape the player’s experience.
- **Choice-Driven Narrative** – Every decision, from minor interactions to major turning points, influences Rahim’s journey.
- **In-Game Phone** – Access essential information, including the map and radio stations.
